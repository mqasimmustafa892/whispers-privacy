1. Who we are

Whispers in the Dark (“the game”) is a single-player horror game published by Iconicplaygames (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you play the game on iPhone or iPad, who collects it, and the choices you have.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so you can continue where you left off:

  • the level you are playing and the levels you have finished;
  • in-game progress, such as items you have found and puzzles you have solved;
  • your settings, such as music and sound on or off;
  • a count of how many times you have opened the game. We use it so no ad is shown when the game opens for the very first time.

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. We show three kinds of ads: an ad when the game opens or you return to it, a banner on menu and result screens, and a full-screen ad between levels or screens.

To deliver, measure and, with your consent, personalize those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may collect:

DataWhy Google uses it
Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow trackingPersonalized ads and measurement across apps, where you have allowed tracking and consented
Identifier for vendors (IDFV)Frequency capping and measuring ads within this game
IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (country or city)Showing ads relevant to your region, and fraud prevention
Device and app information: device model, operating system, language, app versionShowing ads that work on your device, and analytics
Ad interactions: ads viewed or tappedMeasuring ad performance and preventing invalid clicks
Diagnostic data from the ads SDK, such as crash logsKeeping the ad service working

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. We don't receive your advertising identifier or IP address from Google. We only see aggregated reports, such as how many ads were shown.

4. Consent and your choices

EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

When you open the game for the first time in these regions, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether Google and its ad partners may use your data for personalized ads. No ads are requested until you have made a choice. If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized. To change your choice at any time, open Settings → Privacy Settings in the game.

Tracking on iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency)

The game can only use your IDFA if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, neither we nor Google can access it, and the ads you see won't be based on tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

  • Change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.

5. Permissions

The game uses the internet only to load ads, and may ask for permission to track (see section 4). It doesn't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location services.

6. Children

Whispers in the Dark is a horror game and is not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and that personal data about them was collected through it, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

7. Retention and deletion

We don't run servers that store your data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side. To remove the progress saved on your device, uninstall the game or clear its app data. Data held by Google is kept and deleted according to Google's own retention policies, and you can manage it through your Google account and the device settings described above.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information held about you, or get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to or limit its use, including for personalized advertising;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • opt out of “selling” or “sharing” for targeted advertising;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

We don't sell personal information. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled through the controls in section 4 or directly with Google. You can still contact us at any time, and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Security and international transfers

Google may process ad data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. It uses safeguards recognized under data protection law for these transfers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

10. Changes to this policy

If we add features or services that change how data is handled, we will update this policy and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the game's App Store listing.
